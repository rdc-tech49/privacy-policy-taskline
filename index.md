## 4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

### 4.1 Data Storage

- **Local Storage:** Tasks, events, and categories are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database
- **Cloud Storage:** Your data is synced to Firebase Firestore (Google's cloud database) only when you explicitly choose to sync
- **Authentication:** Firebase Auth securely handles all account-related information
- **Backup:** Enable iOS iCloud backup or Android cloud backup to protect your data if your device is lost

### 4.2 Data Security

- All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
- Firestore security rules ensure you can only access your own data
- Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted device storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore)
- Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
- We implement industry-standard security practices to protect against unauthorized access

### 4.3 Data Retention

- Your data is retained for as long as your account is active
- If you delete your account, all associated data (tasks, events, user profile) will be permanently deleted within 30 days
- Audit logs (internal tracking) are automatically deleted after 90 days
